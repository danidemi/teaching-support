# Activity 5: Sprint Retrospective

Goal: capture what worked and what didn't, and turn it into durable process guidance.

- Review what was done in the sprint (what shipped, what slipped, any friction you hit).
- Ask the human for their own feedback on the sprint.
- Together, select exactly:
  - one good thing → extract the good practice that should be repeated next sprint.
  - one bad thing → extract the practice that must not be repeated.
- Append both to `references/do_and_donts.md` (append only — never rewrite past entries).
- Keep `do_and_donts.md` short, actionable, and specific. If it's grown too large, compact it,
  keeping only the most meaningful DOs and DON'Ts.
